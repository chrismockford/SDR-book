Sales Automation Great question. The short answer: no — and that's actually one of the cooler bits of the build. 🧵 The research flow runs the Google searches to pull the event info (dates, theme, speakers, company relevance etc.) exactly ONCE per event, then stores all of that in a lookup table. Every lead that comes through after that just reads from the table instead of triggering a fresh Google research job. So the AI isn't running a full research process 400 times for the same webinar — it runs it once, caches the answer, and every lead benefits from the same enriched context. Much faster, much cleaner, and means the personalisation is based on the same source of truth across all your sequences.
